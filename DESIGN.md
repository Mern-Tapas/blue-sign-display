# Design System: BlueSigns

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Calm Counter"**

BlueSigns is a well-lit shop counter. The room is a soft cool grey. Everything the shopper handles sits on a crisp white tray with a fine drawn edge. One blue tag on each screen marks where to act. The system comes from soft-surface dashboard references, but its job is commerce: prices, sizes, delivery dates and fees have to be read at a glance on a phone. Calm is how that trust is earned. There is no visual noise competing with the MRP, the discount or the Pay button.

Density is phone-first and unhurried. Controls are generous pills on one height scale. Cards hold few things, and related items sit 12–16px apart inside them. Hierarchy comes from size and tone, not weight: headings, titles and labels all share weight 500, and display type is set regular with tight tracking. Motion is short and decelerating. It confirms that something happened (added to bag, sheet opened) and never performs for attention.

The same counter now serves the seller. The admin tier is the storefront's room seen from behind: the same grey canvas, white trays, pill controls and blue signal, run denser and wider. Dashboards, tables and charts are read, not browsed, so chrome goes flat and quiet and the data carries the colour. Every figure on an admin screen is demo data and says so.

Every surface is defined by a hairline first and a soft ink-tinted shadow second, so layers stay legible in the dark theme too. Light and dark are both first-class, and every text and fill pair is verified at WCAG 2.2 AA. The blue accent and the light/dark pair are binding brand commitments. The name "BlueSigns" and its logo (public/logo.jpg) are the brand source of truth: the logo is always used exactly as supplied, never recolored or redrawn, and its blue and yellow define the palette.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cool grey canvas, white trays, hairline edges on every tier.
- One blue signal per view; selection is always a soft blue fill with a 2px blue ring.
- Pill controls on a shared 28/32/40/48/56px scale; rows on 36/44/48/56px.
- Generous corners that step down as surfaces nest (28 → 20 → 14px).
- Weight 500 is the only emphasis step; prices use tabular figures.
- Short, decelerating motion that becomes fades under reduced motion.
- Charts draw from a six-slot categorical palette bound to the entity, never to rank.
- Every chart ships a table twin, and every admin figure is labelled demo data.

## Colors

A cool, near-neutral grey family carries the whole interface. Signs Blue is the single saturated voice, and status colours appear only as soft tints with strong ink.

### Primary
- **Signs Blue** (`signs-blue`): the one primary action per view (Add to bag, Place order, Pay), checked controls, active carousel dots, progress fills and the focus ring. It darkens on hover (`signs-blue-hover`) and again on press (`signs-blue-pressed`). In both themes hover is always darker than rest, so white text stays at or above 4.5:1. The dark theme uses `night-blue` for fills and `night-blue-ink` for blue text.
- **Blue Mist** (`signs-blue-soft` with `signs-blue-soft-ink`): soft buttons, the current docs nav item, positive nudges ("You save ₹1,240"), and the fill of every selected card, row or filter chip.
- **Logo Blue** (`logo-blue`): the exact background of the BlueSigns logo. Chart slot 1, the ordinal and sequential ramps and the diverging positive pole use it; action fills use Signs Blue, one hair darker, so white labels clear 4.5:1 (the logo blue gives 4.41:1).

### Secondary
- **Signal Yellow** (`signal-yellow`): the logo's mark and wordmark yellow, exposed as the `secondary` tokens for brand moments. It is never text or an outline on light surfaces (1.53:1 on white); fills carry Graphite Ink text (11.7:1), and yellow-family text uses `signal-yellow-ink`. Core components don't use it, so Signs Blue stays the one signal. Chart slot 6 is its deeper step.

### Neutral
- **Counter Grey** (`counter-grey`): the page canvas behind everything. White trays read against it without heavy shadows.
- **Tray White** (`tray-white`): cards, header bar, fields, menus and popovers.
- **Inset Mist** (`inset-mist`): nested panels inside cards (price totals, courier details), table header bands and slider tracks. In the dark theme insets sit one step *lighter* than their parent (`night-inset`).
- **Night Panel** (`night-panel`): contrast feature panels such as the footer and the announcement strip, in the light theme. The dark theme lifts it to `night-contrast` so it still separates from the canvas.
- **Graphite Ink** (`graphite-ink`): primary text and icons. It is also the inverse surface for the neutral button, tooltips, the current page chip and selected size pills.
- **Slate Muted** (`slate-muted`): secondary text, captions, placeholders and meta. It passes 4.5:1 on canvas, tray and inset.
- **Pewter Subtle** (`pewter-subtle`): decorative separators and breadcrumb chevrons only. Never readable text.
- **Hairlines** (`hairline`, `hairline-strong`, `divider`, `control-outline`, `control-outline-strong`): one ink hue (rgb 5 24 39) at 7–20% alpha. `divider` separates rows, `control-outline` edges secondary buttons and inputs, `control-outline-strong` marks hover, and `hairline` / `hairline-strong` sit inside the elevation tiers.
- **Night ladder** (`night-canvas` → `night-surface` → `night-inset` → `night-raised` → `night-hover` → `night-contrast`): the dark theme's surfaces. Each step is a visible layer (canvas→surface 1.15:1, surface→raised 1.21:1).

### Status
- **Savings Green** (`savings-green`, `savings-green-soft`, `savings-green-ink`): in stock, delivered, discounts and trend up. White text on the solid fill passes at 5.05:1.
- **Stock Amber** (`stock-amber`, `stock-amber-soft`, `stock-amber-ink`): low stock, pending and expiring points. Text on solid amber is dark, never white.
- **Sale Red** (`sale-red`, `sale-red-soft`, `sale-red-ink`): sale badges, destructive actions, errors and failed payments.
- **Info Blue** (`info-blue`, `info-blue-soft`, `info-blue-ink`): neutral information only.
- **Rating Amber** (`rating-amber`): star fills, at 3:1 or better on white.

### Chart
Data colour is its own family, tuned so adjacent series stay apart for colour-vision deficiency as well as normal vision (worst adjacent CVD ΔE 9.2 light / 9.4 dark; normal-vision ΔE ≥ 19.3). The dark theme swaps in lighter, less saturated twins of the same six hues.
- **Categorical slots 1–6** (`chart-1` blue, `chart-2` aqua, `chart-3` orange, `chart-4` purple, `chart-5` magenta, `chart-6` yellow): series identity, always in this order. `chart-other` (grey) carries "Other" and de-emphasised history such as a sparkline's past.
- **Ordinal ramp** (`chart-ord-1` → `chart-ord-5`): five blue steps for ordered stages (funnels), light to dark in the light theme and reversed in the dark one.
- **Sequential ramp** (`chart-seq-1` → `chart-seq-7`): seven blue steps for magnitude across a grid (heatmaps).
- **Diverging** (`chart-div-neg` orange, `chart-div-mid` neutral, `chart-div-pos` blue): change around a neutral midpoint.
- **Plot furniture** (`chart-grid`, `chart-axis`): gridlines sit one step lighter than the axis; both are 1px and crisp-edged. No frame, no tick marks.

Slots 2, 5 and 6 sit below 3:1 against the light surface, so colour is never the only key: a chart with ≥2 series always carries a legend or direct labels, and every chart carries its table view.

### Named Rules
**The Entity Slot Rule.** A series keeps its slot for its whole life: colour identifies the entity, never its rank. Filtering a chart never repaints the survivors, and a seventh series folds into Other rather than cycling back to slot 1.

**The One Signal Rule.** Only one blue-filled action per view. Secondary actions are white-with-hairline, neutral or ghost. If two things are blue, neither is the signal.

**The Ink-Only Hairline Rule.** Every light-theme line and shadow is mixed from the same ink (rgb 5 24 39). Never introduce a new grey or pure black for borders or shadows.

**The Muted-Not-Faded Rule.** Secondary text uses Slate Muted. Never lower opacity or use Pewter Subtle for anything a shopper needs to read.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geist Sans (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Geist Sans (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (with ui-monospace), for coupon codes, AWB numbers and token names only

**Character:** One neutral grotesque carries everything. Display sizes are set in regular weight with tight tracking, so large headings feel light and calm. A muted second phrase ("Shop by *category*") adds tone without another line of chrome.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400; 56px / 60px, 44px / 48px, 36px / 40px; tracking −3.5% to −2.5%): hero headlines and page titles (h1 uses display-lg), usually with a muted second phrase.
- **Headline** (500; 28px / 34px, 22px / 28px, 18px / 24px): store section headings (28px), dialog and sheet titles (22px), and steps or panels inside a flow such as checkout (18px).
- **Title** (500; 16px / 22px; −1.1%): section titles inside cards and panels.
- **Body** (400; 16px / 24px lead, 14px / 20px default): running text. Paragraphs are capped at about 62ch in docs and copy blocks.
- **Body strong** (500; 14px / 20px): list-item titles, names and key values such as order IDs.
- **Label** (500; 13px / 18px): buttons, field labels, tabs and nav.
- **Caption** (400 or 500; 12px / 16px): meta, hints, timestamps, badges and counts.
- **Overline** (500; 12px / 16px; +2%): table headers and nav group labels only.
- **Figures** (500; 40px / 44px, 30px / 36px; tabular numerals): prices and stats. The currency symbol and MRP step back in Slate Muted.

### Named Rules
**The One Weight Rule.** Weight 500 is the only emphasis step. No semibold or bold in running UI. If something needs more emphasis, it needs a larger role.

**The No Eyebrow Rule.** Never put an overline, kicker or tag above a heading. The heading and its muted phrase carry the section.

**The Tabular Price Rule.** Every price, count, countdown and total uses tabular figures, so numbers don't jitter as they change.

## Layout

The storefront sits in a 1320px container and the docs in 1440px, both with a 24px gutter. Spacing follows a 4px unit. Inside a card, related items sit 12–16px apart and groups 20–24px. Between cards on a page the gap is 16–20px, and store sections are separated by a fluid 56–80px section gap. Related home sections (product rails, offers plus deal) share a tighter 48px group.

Card padding is 20px on phones and 24px from 640px. The header is a floating pill bar 64px tall with a 16px top inset. From 1024px it is a three-column grid (brand and delivery · centred nav · actions), so the nav stays truly centred. Phones get a menu, brand, search and bag in the bar, a delivery bar below it, and a fixed bottom navigation. Consent and connection notices stack in one bottom dock above that bottom nav.

Controls share one height scale (28/32/40/48/56px) and rows another (36/44/48/56px, the last for comfortable table rows), so anything placed side by side lines up. Touch targets under 40px expand to 44px on coarse pointers without changing layout.

The admin tier uses the full window instead of a container. A flat sidebar (256px, 72px collapsed to icons from 1024px, a sheet below that) sits against the canvas, a 64px top bar holds search and account actions, and the page pads to the 24px gutter with a 24px top and 64px bottom. Page content stacks on a 24px rhythm: a page header (breadcrumbs, heading-lg title, inline meta, actions), then filters, then the table or dashboard. KPI tiles step 1 → 2 → 4 columns, and dashboard cards keep a 16px gap. Inline meta under a title uses the meta-list utility, which draws its separators as pseudo-elements so a wrapped row never leaves a dangling dot.

### Named Rules
**The Shared Rail Rule.** Controls that sit side by side use the same height token. A 40px button never sits beside a 44px field.

## Elevation & Depth

BlueSigns is hairline-first. Every tier starts with a 1px ink edge, and a soft ink-tinted shadow adds depth second. That keeps surfaces crisp on the grey canvas and legible in dark mode, where every tier also gets a 1px top highlight. Only content floats: toolbars, filter bars, grouped lists and docs frames sit flat. Content cards rest at the card tier, lift one step on hover, and overlays use popover or modal depth.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Flat** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.08)`): toolbars, header chrome inside pages, grouped lists, insets and docs frames.
- **XS** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.08), 0 1px 2px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.06)`): the raised active segment in segmented controls and tabs.
- **Card** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.08), 0 1px 2px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.04), 0 4px 14px -6px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.08)`): resting content cards, the header bar and price details.
- **Card hover** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.12), 0 2px 4px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.05), 0 14px 28px -12px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.16)`): interactive cards on hover, with a 2px lift (pointer devices only).
- **Popover** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.12), 0 2px 6px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.06), 0 16px 36px -12px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.2)`): menus, selects, popovers and toasts.
- **Modal** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.12), 0 8px 20px -8px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.12), 0 32px 72px -20px rgb(5 24 39 / 0.3)`): dialogs and sheets.

The admin frame follows the same tiering with a harder edge: the sidebar and top bar are flat surfaces separated by a hairline border rather than a shadow, tables sit in a flat grouped list, and only the sticky bulk-action bar (an inverse pill that slides up when rows are selected) and the detail panel reach overlay depth.

### Named Rules
**The Only Content Floats Rule.** Chrome and toolbars are flat, content cards float at card depth, and nothing but overlays reaches popover depth.

**The No Bare Border Rule.** A surface is never edged with a plain CSS border. It gets its edge from its elevation tier. Plain borders are reserved for controls (inputs, secondary buttons, chips).

## Shapes

The form language is soft and concentric. Controls, chips, nav and badges are full pills (9999px). Cards, dialogs and sheets are generously rounded (28px). Selectable cards, alerts and popovers use 24px, insets 20px, and menu items, tooltips and list images 14px. Swatches and thumbnails use 10px, checkboxes and kbd 6px. Radii step down as surfaces nest (card 28 → inset 20 → item 14), and pills nest pills. Icons are lucide outlines at stroke 1.75, sized 14/16/18/20px, sitting in round icon tiles when they accompany a title.

### Named Rules
**The Step-Down Rule.** A nested surface never repeats its parent's radius. Step down at least one level.

## Components

### Buttons
Soft, precise, unhurried: a pill that darkens or tints slightly, presses to 97%, and never changes shape.
- **Shape:** full pill (9999px) at 32 / 40 / 48 / 56px heights. Icons are 14 / 16 / 18 / 20px.
- **Primary:** Signs Blue with white label text (13–16px, weight 500), 20px side padding at 40px. One per view.
- **Hover / Pressed:** primary darkens to `signs-blue-hover`, then `signs-blue-pressed`. Other variants use a currentColor state layer (6% on hover, 12% pressed) plus a 0.97 press scale over 160ms ease-out.
- **Focus:** a solid 2px ring (Signs Blue in light, Blue Ink in dark) with a 2px gap.
- **Secondary:** Tray White with a control-outline hairline. **Neutral:** Graphite Ink with white text (inverse). **Soft:** Blue Mist. **Ghost:** transparent. **Danger:** Sale Red. **Inverse:** white on photography or colour panels.
- **Disabled / Loading:** disabled gets a neutral fill (`disabled-fill`, `disabled-ink`), never an opacity fade. Loading keeps the variant colour, shows a spinner and sets aria-busy.
- **Icon buttons:** circular, on the same scale. A label is required and becomes aria-label and a title.
- **Text buttons:** link-styled actions that don't navigate ("Size chart", "Change"). Blue ink, underline on hover, 44px touch target.

### Chips
- **Style:** pill, Tray White with a control-outline hairline (or Inset Mist for the sunken variant), label text, 28/32/40px heights.
- **State:** selected chips get the selection language (Blue Mist fill, 2px blue ring, blue ink, optional check). Removable chips add a small circular remove button. Chip groups use arrow-key roving focus.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 28px.
- **Background:** Tray White (night-surface in dark). Contrast and accent variants exist for feature panels.
- **Shadow Strategy:** content cards at card depth; toolbars and grouped lists flat; interactive cards lift to card-hover. Destructive zones use a danger-tinted hairline, not a red border.
- **Internal Padding:** 20px, 24px from 640px; 16px for the compact size.
- **Insets:** nested panels in Inset Mist, 20px radius, flat hairline, 12–16px padding. Tinted insets (blue, green, amber, red) carry status without borders.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** pill shell (40px), Tray White with a control-outline hairline; the sunken variant is Inset Mist with no stroke. Multi-line fields use a 20px radius. Placeholders are Slate Muted.
- **Focus:** a 2px ring drawn on the field's own edge.
- **Error / Disabled:** invalid fields take a Sale Red border and their focus ring turns red, with the message announced below. Disabled fields take a neutral fill with no stroke. Select, Combobox and DatePicker triggers behave exactly like inputs.
- **Labels:** every control sits in a Field that wires the label, hint, error and aria attributes. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk.
- **Announcement:** the error carries `role="alert"`, so it is heard when it appears, and the hint's id leaves `aria-describedby` while the error stands in its place. A form-level summary sets `announce="off"` on its fields so a failed submit reads one list rather than every field at once.
- **Grouped choices:** a set of radios, checkboxes or selectable cards sits in a FieldGroup — a fieldset with a legend, because one `label for` can only point at one control. The group carries the hint and the error; each option inherits only the invalid state it has to paint.
- **Read-only vs disabled:** read-only keeps the text readable and the field focusable on a sunken fill; disabled is the neutral dead fill. They are not interchangeable.

### Numbers, money & codes
- **Numbers** are typed as text and clamped on blur, not on the keystroke, so a value on its way to something valid is never fought. Arrow keys step, Shift steps ten, Home and End reach the bounds. A field only claims `role="spinbutton"` when it shows the − / + buttons that promise implies.
- **Money** carries the currency symbol inside the shell (from `Intl`, not hard-coded) and fixes its own decimals, so "rupees or paise" is answered by the component rather than by each screen. Figures are tabular.
- **Codes** (GSTIN, HSN, card) use an append-only mask: it re-formats from the start and parks the caret at the end. Fields that need editing in the middle stay unmasked with a validator.

### Collections
- **Tags:** free text becomes chips on Enter or a comma; Backspace on an empty field removes the last; pasting a separated list splits it. Every add and remove is announced, because the input clears itself and a chip appearing elsewhere is silent.
- **Repeated rows and media** reorder by button first, with pointer drag layered on top. A dragged row lifts to the drag elevation and the drop target takes a 2px accent ring.
- **Generated values** (a handle from a name) follow their source until someone edits them, then stop for good and offer to re-derive. A URL that changes under a link is worse than one that is slightly stale.

### Selection (signature)
Addresses, payment methods, delivery slots, filter chips and table rows share one selected language: a Blue Mist fill with a 2px Signs Blue ring drawn inside the edge, plus a radio dot where it is a single choice. Size pills and segmented controls are the exception and use the solid inverse fill (Graphite Ink). Side bars, 1px blue borders and bold-only selection are not used.

### Navigation
- **Store header:** a floating white pill bar (64px, card depth).
  - The nav group is an Inset Mist pill; the active link is an inverse pill (Graphite Ink, white label).
  - Search appears as a field from 1280px and as an icon button below that.
  - Wishlist and bag are icon buttons with blue count badges; the account chip shows the avatar, adding name and email from 1280px.
  - Theme choice lives in the account menu and the footer.
- **Phones:** a fixed bottom nav (Home, Categories, Wishlist, Bag, Account) with counts, a category sheet and a full-screen search overlay.
- **Menus:** 36px rows with a highlight tint and a row focus ring; long menus scroll within the viewport.
- **Docs sidebar:** 40px pill rows. The current page is Blue Mist, and component counts sit in quiet badges.

### Price Display (signature)
The selling price is large and tabular, with the rupee symbol muted. The struck-through MRP follows in Slate Muted, then the percentage off in Savings Green, then "Inclusive of all taxes" as a caption. Fees (delivery ₹49, platform ₹9, COD ₹19) are always itemised in Price details before payment.

### Charts (signature)
In-house SVG, no chart library, and the scale and format helpers stay server-safe. Every chart is a figure inside a Card that owns the title, the optional legend and a chart/table toggle.
- **Marks:** lines at 2px with round joins and a 4px end point; bars capped at 24px thickness with a 4px rounded data end, a square baseline end and a 2px surface gap between touching bars and stack segments; donut and share-bar segments keep the same 2–3px gap.
- **Furniture:** one value axis with four "nice" ticks, gridlines at `chart-grid`, the zero line at `chart-axis`, category labels in caption. All numbers are tabular.
- **States:** hover or focus dims the other series to 55%, a floating readout gives values first and labels second, and a polite live region speaks the focused point. Refreshing holds the previous render at 50% opacity.
- **Accessibility:** the plot is `role="img"` with a written summary, arrow keys walk the points, and the table view repeats the data in real table semantics. Empty states replace the plot, never a blank grid.

### Admin Shell
- **Sidebar:** grouped links on 40px pill rows; the current page is Blue Mist with blue ink; counts ride as quiet badges, becoming a 2px blue dot when collapsed. Group labels are overlines and become a short hairline when collapsed.
- **Top bar:** 64px, flat with a hairline under it; a sunken pill search button showing a Ctrl K key cap opens the command palette, and a "Demo admin" warning badge sits beside the account actions.
- **Skip link:** the first tab stop in both the store and the admin shell.
- **Command palette:** a dialog holding a combobox and listbox; arrows move the active option via aria-activedescendant, Enter opens, Esc closes and focus returns to the trigger.

### Data Table
- **Structure:** a real table in a flat grouped list. Header cells are overlines on a sunken band; sortable headers are pill buttons carrying aria-sort.
- **Rows:** compact rows use the 36px row token; selection is a checkbox column, and selecting rows raises a sticky inverse pill bar with the count and bulk actions.
- **Controls:** a toolbar above the table holds search and filter menus, then density and column-visibility menus. Filters live above the table, never inside a chart header.
- **States:** skeleton rows while loading, a centred empty state, an error state with a retry, and a card layout per row on phones.

### Metrics
- **KPI tile:** a 16px-padded card holding a label, a figure-lg value, a signed delta and an optional sparkline. The delta pairs an arrow with words and takes green or red from whether the direction is good, never from the sign alone.
- **Meter:** an 8px pill track in the soft tone of its severity with a solid fill, labelled with its value against its limit and exposing role="meter".
- **Status pill:** a dot plus words at 24px; the dot never carries the meaning alone.
- **Activity feed:** a vertical hairline rail with round 28px tone tiles, actor, action and time.

### Named Rules
**The Table Twin Rule.** Every chart ships a table view and, from two series up, a legend or direct labels. If the only way to read a value is to hover, the chart is not finished.

**The One Axis Rule.** One value axis per chart. Two different units in one plot become two charts or a table.

**The Demo Data Rule.** Every admin figure is demo data and says so on the surface that shows it — a badge in the shell, a note in the card, a line in the export. Demo data is generated from a fixed seed and a fixed today, so it never drifts between server and client.

**The Announced Error Rule.** An error that is only visible is only half shown. Every field error is announced where it appears, or deferred to one summary that is announced on submit — never both, and never neither.

**The Button-First Reorder Rule.** Anything that can be reordered is reorderable by button, and announced when it moves. Drag is an enhancement for pointers, because HTML5 drag events never fire on touch — a drag-only list is a list phone users cannot reorder.

**The Honest Surface Rule.** A "checking" spinner, a "creating" row and an upload progress bar are surfaces the design system provides; the debounce, the cancellation and the race behind them belong to the application. The system never implies work it is not doing.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep one Signs Blue action per view and make every other action secondary, neutral or ghost.
- **Do** mark selected cards, rows and chips with Blue Mist plus a 2px blue ring; use the solid inverse fill only for size pills and segmented controls.
- **Do** edge surfaces with their elevation tier's hairline, content cards at card depth and toolbars flat.
- **Do** step radii down when nesting (28px card → 20px inset → 14px item).
- **Do** use Slate Muted for secondary text and tabular figures for every price and count.
- **Do** keep controls that sit side by side on the same height token (32/40/48px) and rows on 36/44/48/56px.
- **Do** give icon-only controls a label, and keep touch targets at 44px on coarse pointers.
- **Do** check every new text/fill pair in both themes for WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 text, 3:1 non-text and focus).
- **Do** label illustrative ratings, reviews, offers and figures as demo content, and keep demo data deterministic (fixed seed, fixed today).
- **Do** assign chart colour by entity slot in the fixed order (blue, aqua, orange, purple, magenta, yellow) and fold anything past six into Other.
- **Do** give every chart a table view, and a legend or direct labels as soon as there are two series.
- **Do** separate inline meta with the meta-list utility so wrapped rows never leave a dangling separator.

### Don't:
- **Don't** stack several blue buttons or tint secondary actions blue.
- **Don't** use semibold or bold overrides; weight 500 is the only emphasis step.
- **Don't** put eyebrow labels, overlines or kickers above headings.
- **Don't** edge a card or panel with a bare CSS border, or float chrome at popover depth.
- **Don't** fade text with opacity or Pewter Subtle, or put white text on solid amber.
- **Don't** add decorative gradients, loops or glows to draw attention to merchandising; motion only confirms actions.
- **Don't** repeat a parent's radius on a nested panel.
- **Don't** use a disabled look built from opacity; use the neutral disabled fill.
- **Don't** colour a series by rank, cycle past slot 6, or repaint survivors when a chart is filtered.
- **Don't** put a second value axis, a chart frame, tick marks or a 3D or gradient fill in a plot.
- **Don't** hide a value behind hover only, or let a dot or a colour carry a status without its word.
- **Don't** show an admin figure without its demo-data label.

## Documented exceptions

Three literals sit outside the system on purpose. They are recorded here so the detector's advisories stay meaningful.

- **Merchandise swatch hexes** (`#d9c7a7` sand, `#c83e3e` sold-out demo) in the product docs are product data — the colour of a thing being sold — not interface colour. They never style chrome.
- **10px initials in the `xs` avatar.** The type ramp starts at 12px; a 24px avatar cannot carry it. The initials are decorative (the name is always adjacent or on the label), so they are not a text size the system offers.
- **Comfortable data-table rows are 56px** (`row-xl`), one step above the 48px list row, because a cell carries two lines.
